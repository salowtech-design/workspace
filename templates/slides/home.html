Slides

Kept in this browser only. Text, shapes and pictures, three themes. No sharing. Two kinds of animation, both only in Present: an optional fade between slides, and items that appear or fade in on a click.

Where this deck is stored

Only in this browser, as a separate copy for each signed-in person, which keeps people apart inside the app. It is not sent to the server, not shared, not synced and not a backup. It is not encrypted against someone who can open this browser's storage. It stays on this device after you sign out until you clear it, and the browser can clear it. Use Export (.json) to keep a copy. If the stored deck cannot be read, it is left untouched and editing is paused. Choosing "Start a new deck" first copies the unreadable data to a backup key in this browser. That backup is kept until you choose Start a new deck again, which replaces it, or the browser clears it. Clear deck replaces the current deck with one empty slide, and undo works until you reload; it does not touch a backup. Limits: 50 slides, 30 items per slide, 1000 characters per text.

Your stored deck could not be read (it may be damaged or from a newer version). It has not been changed, and editing is paused so it is not overwritten.

Find and replace

Searches the text of items on the slides. Not the master, picture descriptions or speaker notes. Plain text only: no wildcards. Each replace is one Undo step.

Versions

Named copies of the whole deck (slides, items, master, theme, notes), kept in this browser only, up to 5. Not a backup: they live in the same browser storage as the deck. Pictures are not copied into a version; the deck only points to them. Restore replaces the deck on screen. Save the current deck as a version first if you want to keep it: Undo brings it back only until you reload. Delete asks you to confirm and cannot be undone. Clear deck does not remove versions. Versions stay in this browser, under this person, until you delete them or clear the browser's site data. Two open tabs share the list; use one tab at a time.

      Click an item or choose it from the list to select it. With an item selected and focus on the slide, arrow keys move it by 10, Shift plus an arrow key resizes it by 10, and Delete removes it. You can also drag an item to move it, or drag the blue square at its corner to resize it; one drag is one Undo step and Esc cancels it. Duplicate item copies the selected item just above it, 20 further right and down (left and up near the edge), with its text, style and picture; an animation is not copied. It stops at 30 items per slide (10 on the master) and 20 pictures per deck. Items higher in the list are drawn underneath later ones; use Send backward and Bring forward to change that. Bold, italic and Align (default is left for text items and centre for shapes) are drawn on the canvas, in Present and in the PDF. A deck that uses any of them is saved in a newer format that an older copy of this page will report as unreadable instead of dropping the styling. Text wrapping is approximate (bold is estimated wider): it works by character, not by joined emoji or accent sequences, so those can split across lines. While you drag or resize an item, its edges and middle snap to the slide's edges and middle and to the other items' edges and middle when within 6 units, and a dashed line shows what it lined up with. Hold Alt to drag without snapping. Arrow keys and the number fields never snap.

      This deck has pictures. Export (.json) saves references only, so the pictures would be missing elsewhere. Use Export with pictures to include them. The file includes speaker notes. The PDF has one page per slide, uses Helvetica, and leaves out speaker notes. Characters Helvetica cannot draw stop the export with a message.

      Turning this on shows your notes on this screen. Anyone who can see or share this screen, including an audience, will see them. This is not a private presenter view. It starts off each time you present.

      Forward: Right arrow, Down arrow, Page Down, Space and Enter show the next animated item, or the next slide when none is left. Back: Left arrow, Up arrow, Page Up and Backspace hide the last item shown, or return to the previous slide. Home goes to the first slide and End to the last slide with all its items shown. Showing a deck does not change it.

      A slide can have its own background colour from this list. A colour is offered only while the text on the slide stays readable on it (contrast of at least 4.5). A change that would break that is refused with a message, and switching theme asks first and puts the affected slides back to the theme background. The colour is drawn in the editor, in Present and in the PDF. A deck that uses one is saved in a newer format (version 8) that an older copy of this page reports as unreadable instead of dropping the colour. The master has no background of its own.

      A fade is a short fade-in when you move to this slide in Present. It does not run on the first slide shown, in the editor or in the PDF, and it does not run if your device asks for reduced motion. The fade speed is slow, normal or fast; normal is what every earlier deck uses and stores nothing. A deck that uses transitions is saved in the newer deck format, and an older copy of this page leaves such a deck untouched instead of reading it.

      Animation

        No animated items on this slide.

        In Present, an animated item is hidden until its step. Each item plays once, in the order listed, and slide items only (not master items). Forward keys (Right or Down arrow, Page Down, Space, Enter) and the Next button show the next step at once, and only move to the next slide when no step is left. Back keys (Left or Up arrow, Page Up, Backspace) and the Previous button hide the last step shown first. A step set to After previous plays by itself 1 second after the step before it, or 1 second after the show opens or the slide appears when it is first. The wait is fixed, any key press restarts it, going back or jumping with Home or End never starts it, and moving to the next slide is always a key press or click. The editor and the PDF show every item, with no animation. Fade in does not run if your device asks for reduced motion; the item then just appears (After previous timing still applies). There is no separate preview: use Present from this slide to try it. A deck that uses animation is saved as format version 6 (7 when an item plays after previous), which older versions of this page refuse to open rather than drop the animation.

        Master

        The master holds up to 10 text items and shapes that are drawn under every slide that shows it, in the editor, in Present and in the PDF. Placeholder words in the master are ordinary text too. Master items cannot be selected on a slide: choose Edit master to change them. A deck with a master is saved in a newer deck format (Export includes the master). An older copy of this page cannot read such a deck: it leaves it untouched and pauses editing instead of dropping the master. Clear deck also removes the master. Master text is also checked for PDF characters.

        Pictures: PNG or JPEG only, up to 2 MB each, up to 4096 pixels on a side and 12 million pixels, 20 pictures per deck, 10 MB of pictures per deck and 48 million pixels of pictures per deck (the PDF maker holds each decoded picture in memory, about 25 MB per 12 million pixels as measured here, so a deck over this is refused when making a PDF). The picture bytes stay in this browser (separately from the deck text, and per signed-in person) and are never sent to the server. The deck holds only a fingerprint of each picture. If the browser clears its site data, the deck stays readable and each missing picture is drawn as a "Picture missing" box in the editor, in Present and in the PDF. The same picture file is kept once. This page never deletes picture files, so Undo, Redo and Clear deck cannot lose one. The consequence is residual local storage: picture files that no deck uses any more stay in this browser until you clear its site data. There is no cleanup feature. Deleting a picture item, a slide or a deck does not erase its file, and nothing here promises secure deletion. A deck that uses a picture is saved in a newer deck format that an older copy of this page reports as unreadable. Export (.json) holds only references, not the pictures: another device would show "Picture missing" boxes. Export with pictures (.json) includes the picture bytes, so the file can be large (about a third bigger than the pictures) and anyone with the file can see them. Pictures are fitted inside their box without stretching. Alt text: write a description in the Text box for the selected picture.

        A layout only places ordinary text items on the new slide. The placeholder words (Title, Subtitle, Content) are ordinary text: replace or delete them, or they appear in Present and in the PDF. A slide made without the link box ticked is never changed by a layout edit. A slide made with it ticked is linked: Edit layout below moves the linked items, except a value you changed on the slide yourself (a value you set back to the layout value follows the layout again). Unlink this slide keeps what it shows. A layout is not the master: items that should repeat on every slide belong in the master below.

        Drive

        Saves deck.json, the same file as Export (.json), for a deck without pictures. A deck with pictures is not saved yet. Save puts a file at the top of your own My Drive (Drive records its name, size and checksum); the file itself stays in this browser on this device. Only you can see it until you share it in Drive, and anyone you share it with gets the record but not the file. Open lists your own top-level Drive files whose copy is on this device and uses Import, which asks before it replaces anything.
