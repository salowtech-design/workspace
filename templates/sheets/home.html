Sheets

Kept in this browser only. Formulas start with =. Subset of spreadsheet features, no sharing.

Supported functions and limits

Functions: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, ABS, ROUND, LEN, UPPER, LOWER, CONCAT, IF, AND, OR, NOT. Operators + - * / ^ & % and comparisons. This is a subset, not full Excel or Google Sheets compatibility. The visible grid is A1 to H20. Errors: #DIV/0!, #VALUE!, #NAME?, #REF!, #CIRC!, #PARSE!, #LIMIT! (too deep or too much work).

Where this sheet is stored

Only in this browser, as a separate copy for each signed-in person, which keeps people apart inside the app. It is not sent to the server, not shared, not synced and not a backup. It is not encrypted against someone who can open this browser's storage. It stays on this device after you sign out until you clear it, and the browser can clear it. Use Export (.json) to keep a copy.

Arrow keys move, Enter moves down, Esc cancels an edit, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo.
